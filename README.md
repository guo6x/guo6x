## Current Research

### Omni-Context

**Evidence-Qualified and Revision-Aware Decision Intelligence for Long-Lived AI Agents**

Omni-Context explores how long-lived AI agents can:

- determine whether retrieved information is still valid and relevant;
- decide whether available evidence is sufficient before acting;
- respect constraints, authority, and approval boundaries;
- revise, reverse, or invalidate earlier decisions when evidence or outcomes change; and
- remain auditable through explicit evidence and decision lineage.

Repository: https://github.com/guo6x/Omni-context
