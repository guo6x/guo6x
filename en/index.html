North star / Long-term mission

Aim far.
Stay grounded.

Aerospace is not a concept render or a polished demo. It requires capital, industrial collaboration, test evidence, safety boundaries and sustained work. For now, I begin with concrete needs: AI products that can actually save users time or money, followed by progressively deeper work in robotics, engineering systems and aerospace.

I want to build more than another AI app. Over time, I hope to connect intelligence, computation, controls and manufacturing into engineering capabilities that work in the real world.

01 / DISCOVER

Find real problems

Look for repetitive work, avoidable costs and errors that matter to actual users.

02 / BUILD

Build verifiable tools

Make software deliverable, testable and responsive to real feedback.

03 / LEARN

Develop the engineering base

Keep studying controls, manufacturing, simulation, embedded systems and integration.

FIELD / 01 / CONCEPT VISUAL
Aerospace engineering / Flight controls

AI Rocket · Ad Astra

An experimental software–hardware exploration of small rockets and EDF thrust-vector control: STM32 firmware, telemetry, a Python ground station, aerodynamic and dynamics simulations, and deterministic offline replay. The objective is to build repeatable engineering evidence, not claim a launch that has not happened.

STM32 / PythonControl & SimulationGround Station
Evidence level: offline software tests. Physical-system, manufacturing and flight qualification remain incomplete.

SYSTEM / 02 / CONCEPT VISUAL
Evidence-aware AI / Long-lived agents

Omni-Context

Research and engineering around evidence qualification, memory validity and decision lineage for long-running AI agents. The critical question: how do we distinguish a successful tool call from an outcome that actually occurred, and how does the agent revise a prior decision?

Agent MemoryEvidence QualificationTraceability
Evidence level: active research engineering baseline; no claim of formal performance proof or frozen product.

From practical AI to aerospace / The path

One direction.
Many possible routes.

I don't already run an aerospace company, and I don't assume there is one predetermined business model. Building commercially useful AI products before moving into deeper physical engineering is the most practical path I can work on today.

NOW

Validate value with AI

Find substantial user pain points and test whether tools save time, money or errors.

BUILD

Reliable engineering systems

Connect software, controls, mechanical design and testing while documenting failed assumptions and limitations.

EXPAND

Teams, resources, partners

Seek practical collaborations and engineering applications rather than simply accumulating more projects.

NORTH STAR

Broader space exploration

As capabilities and resources mature, explore credible ways to contribute to the aerospace industry.

This is an evolving strategy, not a set of completed milestones or a funding/timeline promise.
